## Reason

New Article 13, Case law. Significant decisions are filed as numbered (C-NNNN), labeled cases with a headnote and holding, in three courts (chief < assembly < steward). Stare decisis: members check and cite precedent, and depart only by distinguishing or by overruling before a court of equal or higher rank. Holdings are frozen and fingerprinted; history is append-only. The citator derives status; 90-day reviews; precedent survival rate in the digest; public; recorded as events; void where inconsistent with the Charter. Article 9.1 authority order now includes case law. Added case.py and tests; the Chief is Reporter; case law in force is injected into every agent prompt; read-only case search tools for members (Steward-ratified tool change under 7.6); gov-publish publishes cases. Seed cases C-0001 to C-0005 codify the launch sequence, public governance (A-0001), the Research Library rule (A-0002), replayability (A-0003), and case law itself (A-0004).
